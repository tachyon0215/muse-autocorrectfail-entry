# muse-autocorrectfail-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Funniest Autocorrect Fail](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-funniest-autocorrect-fail-ddf82154).

## The entry

> Dear Satan, thank you for the bicycle. I promise I was good all year, just like you asked in the mall.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: The slip (Santa to Satan) is unmistakable on first read, and the child's innocent politeness - thanking him, citing the mall promise - is what makes the wrong word land.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 be9fa7f30e3b93271d2b96e9855e42baacf457112885ce5a0e03cd6f27882bd5).
